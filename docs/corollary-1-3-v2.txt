Inputs are exactly fixed degree d>=3, fixed channel count k>=1, fixed strict
bulk E, and a deterministic width delta. Delta is nonnegative and tends to
zero only along M>d with M*d even. There is no monotonicity, rate, tuple
existence, compact-window, model-realization, BS/HY, moment or Gaussianity
premise. The graph law is the literal uniform law on labelled simple
regular graphs. The limit is in graph probability.

Quantity being controlled:
  fixedEnergyStarWassersteinSupReal hd E hE g (delta M)
is the supremum over ALL ordered real orthonormal eigenvector tuples A with
  forall a, abs(A.eigenvalue a-E)<=delta M
of the physical Euclidean W2 distance from A's actual empirical star law
to the SAME product Gaussian star measure at E. The target is independent of
M, graph and candidate. Empty suprema are zero. All choices of signs,
rotations in repeated eigenspaces, and exact neighbor enumerations occur in
the full GraphEigenCandidate type.

Proof blueprint and implemented chain
-------------------------------------
1. Fix a compact bulk neighborhood K of E. The shrinking window lies inside
   K eventually. All this is constructed internally from strict bulk E.
2. Combine proved common BS classification events and uniform scalar fourth
   control. The complement of their intersection has probability tending to
   zero. The fourth bound applies to every channel for ANY fixed k; there
   is no k<=4 hypothesis in the corollary.
3. If a positive fixed W2 error persists, extract a subsequence of bad good
   graphs and choose one witnessing candidate on each. Choices are made
   only where a witness exists. No tuple is needed on empty windows or at
   sizes smaller than k.
4. Every selected eigenvalue tends to E by its window bound. The joint BS
   theorem and uniform fourth control give the actual empirical star laws
   a common Gaussian weak limit, with no loss of second moments. The proved
   transport theorem upgrades this to W2 convergence to the fixed product
   Gaussian at E, contradicting the bad threshold.
5. This proves convergence of the existential bad event. To pass to the
   literal supremum, choose 0<eta<epsilon: a supremum >=epsilon must have a
   candidate whose distance exceeds eta. No maximizing tuple is assumed.
6. Finite graph spaces give measurability. The independent coupling and the
   exact unit-coordinate second moments prove, on EVERY graph/candidate,
     W2^2 <= 4*k*(d+1).
   Consequently the entire supremum is finite. The ordinary real version
   uses toReal together with a proved ofReal roundtrip; infinity cannot be
   silently converted to zero.
7. A cyclic jump graph constructs every labelled simple regular graph size
   permitted by M>d and M*d even. Paired short jumps give even degree; for
   odd degree the even vertex count permits an additional antipodal jump.
   Necessity follows from degree<vertex_count and the handshake identity.
   Thus the intrinsic ensemble-size filter equals EXACTLY the article's
   arithmetic filter, and this filter is proved nontrivial.
8. The sequential criterion for countably generated filters promotes the
   sequence theorem to the arithmetic-size endpoint. Width convergence is
   assumed only on this same filter. Finite inadmissible prefixes are
   discarded without a new model condition.
