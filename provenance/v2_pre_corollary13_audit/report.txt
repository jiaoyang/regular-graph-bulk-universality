Verdict
-------
The project contains substantive formal proofs of the core bulk-universality
and mixed-fourth-moment results for the actual uniform simple regular-graph
ensemble. I found no extra statistical assumption or weakened Gaussian,
counting-measure, uniform-graph, or Wasserstein definition in the inspected
final proof paths.

It is NOT a complete formalization of the revised article at the quantifiers,
rates and generality stated throughout V2. The revised Gaussian-star corollary
needs a different uniform endpoint, and several intermediate propositions are
stronger than the concrete results currently proved. Successful Lean checking
does not establish correspondence with a stronger TeX proposition.

A. Principal results
--------------------

A1. Bulk universality, V2 thm:main, lines 137-145: substantive match,
with an explicit arithmetic-admissibility wrapper still missing.

GeneralizedBulk/ProvedRegularGraphUniversality.lean:47-50 defines the actual
finite counting measure from ALL matrix eigenvalues, retaining multiplicities
and the constant mode. The theorem at lines 81-90 gives precisely
  sum_i delta_[N*pi*rawDensity(d,E)*(lambda_i-E)]
under regularGraphLaw, at every fixed strict bulk E and d>=3.
GeneralizedBulk/ExternalInputs.lean:102-106 tests ALL bounded continuous real
functions on the counting-measure space. The vendored PointProcessModel.lean:
24-37 uses locally finite integer counting measures and the actual vague
topology. The finite law is uniform on all labelled simple d-regular graphs;
there is no connectedness, simple-spectrum, or conditioned-subfamily premise.

The final theorem constructs the BS/HY inputs internally. The generic _of_HY
lemmas are intermediate proof tools, not unclosed premises of the public
finite-ensemble result. The response/loop record is also constructed:
GeneralizedBulk/Gaussian/GlobalConstruction.lean:65-88,
GeneralizedBulk/Exterior/ConstructedStationarity.lean:57-114,
GeneralizedBulk/ConstructedCriterion.lean:55-85, and
GeneralizedBulk/RegularGraphUniversality.lean:68-84.

V2 explicitly ranges over N>d with d*N even (line 113). Lean instead defines
AdmissibleSize d M as 0<M and Nonempty (RegularGraph M d), in
Graph/AdmissibleSizeLimits.lean:34-40. Graph/AdmissibleNontrivial.lean:13-43
constructs arbitrarily large clique-block sizes and proves that this filter is
nontrivial. This does NOT prove every size satisfying the numerical conditions
is represented. The finite combinatorial equivalence / numeric-size wrapper
was not found in the project or as an already-applied imported theorem.
This is a missing model-interface proof, not a hidden analytic assumption or
a restriction of the graph ensemble to clique blocks.

A2. Mixed fourth moments, V2 thm:fourth, lines 201-214: match of the
substantive result, subject to the same arithmetic-admissibility interface.

Graph/ArticleMixedFourth.lean:16-28 chooses positive b, chi and C before M,
graph, tuple and four slots, and gives one measurable event with the polynomial
probability and moment bounds at EVERY positive admissible size. It includes
all k=1,...,4, all real orthonormal eigenfamilies and eigenspace rotations,
all repeated physical slots, and eigenvalues anywhere in the compact bulk set.
No nearby-energy condition is added. The tuple pinning and common bad-witness
construction are actual proofs, not assumptions of uniformity.

The empirical law is uniform root times uniform neighbor permutation with
sqrt(N) scaling: GaussianWave/Covariance.lean:160-190. The comparison covariance
is block-diagonal, and its equality with a product of independent Gaussian
stars is proved: GaussianWave/StarComparison.lean:25-27,44-77. The moment
quantity is an actual difference of integrals, Graph/UniformMixedFourth.lean:
14-17. Integrability is established in the supporting finite/Gaussian lemmas.

A3. Revised Gaussian-star corollary, V2 cor:gaussian, lines 227-240:
NOT yet the literal uniform theorem in the project.

V2 fixes one E and takes
  sup_{orthonormal tuples, max_a |lambda_a-E| <= delta_N}
    W2(empirical star law, product Gaussian star law AT E),
with empty supremum zero and convergence in graph probability.

Graph/ArticleWassersteinCorollaries.lean:14-19,40-48 instead proves the OLD
article's supremum: energies in compact K, pairwise diameter at most delta_N,
and Gaussian comparison AT EACH TUPLE'S OWN EIGENVALUES.
Graph/ArticleWassersteinCommonEnergy.lean:140-153 proves a fixed-E conclusion
for a globally selected tuple whose eigenvalues converge in probability to E.
It does not declare or prove V2's fixed-E supremum over the shrinking window.

This looks derivable from the existing deterministic common-energy limit and
uniform good-event / bad-witness machinery. That derivation is still a Lean
obligation; an informal implication or a theorem about one selected family
cannot be reported as an already-checked uniform supremum theorem.

The metric itself is genuine: GaussianWave/Transport.lean:20-24,126-146,
419-421 uses actual couplings, sum of squared physical coordinate differences,
its infimum and square root. This finding is about coverage, not a fake W2.

B. Intermediate entropy propositions do not all match V2
--------------------------------------------------------

B1. Gaussian-noise lower bound, V2 prop:channel, lines 1134-1155:
concrete quantitative bounds are weaker.

V2 sets theta_V=1/[100(k(d+1)+1)], permits signal size 0.5*N^theta_V
and covariance range [N^-theta_V,N^theta_V], and asserts error
C*N^-theta_V*log N with failure C_d*exp(-N^(1-theta_V)).

Entropy/ChannelParameters.lean:14-19 sets
  theta_L=1/[1000(k+1)(d+1)], b_L=theta_L/8.
Graph/GaussianChannelPolynomial.lean:54-71 proves the correlated channel
bound for signal/covariance exponents b_L with error N^(-theta_L/2).
Its good-event parameter is t=N^(1/4) (line 61); the growing-palette event
has the weaker exp(-N^(1/4)) failure estimate (GrowingPaletteGood.lean:13-20).
The concrete theorem is substantive and uniform over its permitted noise and
signals, but it does not prove the displayed V2 rates or full parameter range.
The exact numerical theta cannot simply be renamed to make these equivalent.

B2. High-noise quadratic approximation, V2 prop:quadratic,
lines 1244-1257: concrete generality and B dependence are weaker.

V2 covers arbitrary bounded neighbor-invariant normalized laws and asserts
  ||u_s||_3 <= C*B*s^(3/2),
  |Delta_s - Q(u_s)/2| <= C*B^3*s^(9/2),
for s<=c*B^-2, with constants independent of B.

Entropy/ConstructedEntropyDeficit.lean:35-44 proves a finite-weighted-signal
estimate with remainder
  C*(1+((dim+1)*B)^2)^9 * a^9,
where a=sqrt(s). For fixed dimension this grows like B^18*s^(9/2), rather
than B^3*s^(9/2). Conversion between Euclidean and coordinate-l1 bounds changes
dimension constants, not this power of B. Entropy/UniformSmoothing.lean:45-53
has the same coarse dependence. The finite empirical application is covered;
the arbitrary-law sharp proposition is not thereby proved.

Entropy/ChaosCriterion.lean:150-156 contains the sharp B^(4/3) optimized form,
but assumes ChaosEntropyHypotheses. Its fields explicitly include the Taylor
bound, chaos expansion, coercivity and deficit bounds (lines 38-52). This is
an honestly labelled conditional abstract lemma, not an independent proof of
V2's concrete sharp analytic proposition. The final fourth-moment proof uses
the concrete weaker estimate and still obtains a positive polynomial exponent.

B3. Half-noise deficit / separate-energy OU theorem: literal domain and
output gaps remain.

V2 prop:deficit, lines 1181-1192, gives both nonnegativity and an upper bound
for (X+Z)/sqrt(2), i.e. signal fraction s=1/2. Graph/BulkEntropyDeficit.lean:
18-33 gives an upper bound for amplitudes 0<=a<=1/2, i.e. s=a^2<=1/4.
The amplitude convention is explicit in Entropy/MehlerSignalScale.lean:21,
37-39. The reviewed endpoint is not the same displayed half-noise assertion.

The GaussianWave/BS heat monotonicity and equality theorems are real proofs,
but at one common scalar energy c: BS/ColoredUpperBound.lean:17-25 and
BS/ColoredGaussianStrictness.lean:76-89. V2 lem:ou, lines 959-1009, treats
separate energies lambda_a. The quantitative route uses a separate-energy
Gaussian frame/coercivity argument, rather than an already-identified literal
separate-energy version of this full OU theorem.

B4. Fourth-chaos coercivity is substantively covered.
Entropy/UniformFourthCoercivity.lean:131-137 handles arbitrary separate
energies in a compact bulk set, with actual Gaussian L2 norms and conditional
operators (GaussianFrameSystem.lean:37-46). The kernel/coercivity is proved in
ColoredFourthCoercivity.lean:48-76. Empirical whitening, zero means, identity
covariance, permutation/reversal invariance and admissibility are derived
from actual orthonormal eigenvectors, not supplied as conclusion certificates.

C. Spectral and microscopic propositions
----------------------------------------

C1. V2 local-star error, eq:local-law at line 1047, is N^-1/128.
LocalLaw/ProvedLocalStarInput.lean:51 fixes the public proved exponent at
1/1024, with its numerical estimate in StarPolynomialError.lean:162-165.
N^-1/1024 is weaker for large N. The source theorem is parameterized sufficiently
to permit V2's geometric choices, but a sharper compact-bulk stability/error
adapter is still needed to claim that exact rate.

C2. V2 prop:inputs(d), lines 1056-1059, controls tree neighborhoods of radius
(log_{d-1} N)/8 with at most sqrt(N) exceptions. The public HG record constructs
fixed-radius geometry. The bridge uses a structural radius proportional to
log N/16 with at most N^(1/4) bad centers; this does not by itself give the
larger V2 radius. General source counting estimates provide ingredients for a
retuned quantitative proof, but the exact claimed event was not identified.

C3. V2's constant mesoscopic trace bound IS covered.
ExternalInputs.lean:177-185 requires a constant, and
LocalLaw/ProvedMesoscopicInput.lean:136-148 proves a bound independent of N down
to N^(-1+alpha). The N^alpha term at lines 115-131 only absorbs a logarithmic
source cutoff. It should not be misreported as a weaker N^alpha trace bound.
SourceScaling.lean:37-89 proves the exact raw/normalized resolvent conversion.

C4. V2 prop:tight, lines 2608-2620, states the factorial-type tail
C*exp(-c*t*log(2+t))+C*N^-b. Analysis/ReversibleTail.lean:91-129 deliberately
bounds the genuine C/t recursion by a geometric 1/2 recursion; lines 164-190
derive capped moments. Graph/BulkScalarTightness.lean:19-40 supplies tightness
and subsequences. The stronger displayed factorial-tail endpoint was not found.
The retained C/t recursion may allow a further quantitative iteration proof;
this is a coverage gap, not a claim that the stronger tail is false.

C5. V2 eq:normalization, lines 2685-2686, gives an L1 error <=C*Y^-1/2 for
EVERY real Y>=1. Graph/NormalizationFromRawLimit.lean:13-28 instead gives
L1 convergence to i along integer heights normalizationHeight n=(n+1)*i,
plus infinite total mass. Gaussian/HeightCouplings.lean constructs separate
couplings at each integer height. The precise all-real-height rate is not
an output of these theorems.

C6. V2 prop:marked, lines 2641-2647 and 2693 onward, states locally uniform
joint convergence of analytic fields and one whole-field exchangeable coupling.
Graph/BulkResolventJetConvergence.lean:16-45 supplies every finite family of
values and derivative slots. Whole Gaussian analytic fields are constructed,
but the reviewed convergence/coupling route uses finite jets and individual
height couplings. These suffice for the finite-test loop hierarchy; they do
not constitute the literal stronger function-space/coupling proposition.

C7. The switching acceptance rule is a valid alternative, not literally V2's.
V2 lines 2361-2363 accepts only if the old two edges are the only edges among
all four endpoints. Graph/UniformSwitching.lean:211-213 checks distinctness
and absence of the two proposed new edges, allowing the other two cross edges.
The formal involution/reversibility and rejection estimates are proved for
that broader rule. ExteriorSwitching.lean:211 onward and
Exterior/RawCoordinates.lean:17 retain boundary corrections. This does not
invalidate the formal proof; it is a different implementation of switching.

formula or an explicit intensity-1/pi formula. Those should not be described
as additional formal conclusions of this project.

uniform-ensemble theorem. Loop/Hierarchy.lean:145-191 proves integrability and
the full required finite-test hierarchy, including repeated arguments and
mixed half-planes. Undefined Bochner integrals are not being used to create
a vacuous loop equation. Generic hypotheses in reusable helper lemmas are
not hypotheses of the final proved graph theorem.

F. Correction to previous completeness language and next obligations
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A blanket description of the ENTIRE article as fully formalized was too broad.
The old article already contains the channel theta/failure and B^3 remainder
claims (old source lines 436-441 and 589), so these auxiliary gaps are not all
caused by the revision. The earlier kernel/axiom PASS remains valid for the
encoded statements, and the principal bulk and fourth-moment results are real.
It does not establish every intermediate estimate as written in either article.

To claim complete coverage of V2, the project should at least:
1. Prove arithmetic admissibility equivalence and numeric-size endpoints.
2. Add and prove V2's fixed-E shrinking-window supremum theorem, with empty
   supremum zero and genuine physical W2.
3. Prove the exact channel, sharp quadratic, half-noise/separate-energy heat,
   local-star and logarithmic-radius geometry statements, or explicitly revise
   the article's intermediate statements/proof to the bounds actually proved.
4. Prove the stated factorial tail, all-real-height normalization rate and
   whole-field convergence/coupling propositions, or document the weaker
   finite-test alternative proof as such.
5. Update the article mapping and coverage report to V2's actual source hash
   and labels. Retain a strict distinction between proof-object validation
   and correspondence to the mathematical claims in the revised text.

No source fix or theorem weakening was performed during this review.
